## A world taking shape

My Digital World begins with a guided journey through a forest toward a white sanctuary. After entering, a world map connects public places around an ancient tree.

## What is here

- A procedural forest and architectural entrance.
- A world map with connected paths and selectable landmarks.
- A Gallery whose project pages are generated from Markdown content.

The site is built with **React, Three.js, and React Three Fiber**. Repeated vegetation uses instancing; camera movement is guided rather than freely controlled.

![The Public World Map](/projects/my-digital-world/cover.png)

## Current stage

The entrance and map are implemented. The Gallery content system is the next layer; other destinations remain placeholders or locked landmarks. This existing Gallery entry is retained as completed during the shared-system migration; its date records the catalog entry, not the completion of all future website work.
